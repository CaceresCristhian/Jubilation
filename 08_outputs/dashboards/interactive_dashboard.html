Gender Pension Dimension & Analytical Limitations

Academic Sensitivity Protocol
What Is Explicitly Modeled & Captured
  • Wage & Part-Time Disparities: Estimated adjusted gender wage difference (–11.4%) and higher female part-time shares.
  • Childcare Credits (§ 56 SGB VI): Up to 3.0 Entgeltpunkte (~€127/mo pension) for primary caregiver per child born 1992+.
  • Longevity Differential (Destatis 16. BVB): Life expectancy at 67 is ~21.5 years for females vs ~18.5 years for males.
  • Basic Pension (Grundrente § 76g): Stylized point uplift for low-earners with 33+ qualifying contribution years.
Crucial Boundaries & Unmodeled Variables
  • Survivor Pensions (Witwenrente § 46 SGB VI): Up to 55–60% spousal derived pension rights are not modeled at single level.
  • Divorce Rights Splitting (Versorgungsausgleich): Equalization of pension rights between spouses upon divorce.
  • Intra-Household Wealth Pooling: Married couples often accumulate and consume wealth jointly.
  • Informal Caregiving Credits (§ 3 SGB VI): Pension points accrued while providing home care for family members.

Key Parameters & Institutional Glossary

Detailed explanation of German statutory pension formulas, adequacy thresholds, and economic definitions.

Legal Basis: SGB VI & SGB XII
GRV

Statutory Pension (GRV - SGB VI)

Primary pillar of the German pension system. Workers earn Earnings Points (EP) based on wages relative to the national average (€51,944 in 2026, capped at BBG €101,400). At age 67, EP is multiplied by the Current Pension Value (AR = €42.52/EP), minus statutory health (KVdR 8.75%) and care (PV 3.6%–4.2%) insurance.

NRR

Net Replacement Rate (NRR)

Measures total net retirement income as a percentage of pre-retirement net labor earnings. Common academic pension adequacy benchmarks evaluate 60% as standard baseline maintenance, and 75%+ as a comfortable standard of living.

SGB

Grundsicherung im Alter (SGB XII)

Means-tested socio-cultural subsistence guarantee (Chapter 4, SGB XII). Modeled at an illustrative single-adult benchmark of €1,113/month (€563 Level 1 standard rate + assumed €550 accommodation/heating benchmark) for retirees whose autonomous income falls below this floor and whose liquid savings do not exceed €10,000 (Schonvermögen § 90).

S*

Required Add. Savings (S*)

The additional monthly amount a worker must save/invest from current age until age 67 to bridge the gap to standard replacement adequacy (€1,450–€1,800/mo, based on 60% of net wages). Reaching adequate income reduces this required savings to €0 (Fully Funded).

4%

Private Wealth Drawdown (4% Decumulation Rule)

Stylized monthly withdrawal rate generated from private liquid savings and ETF portfolios. Illustrates cashflow decumulation over a deterministic 20-year retirement horizon (ages 67–87) without exhaustively modeling individual mortality hazards.

OADR

Old-Age Dependency Ratio (OADR)

Ratio of retirement-age population (67+) to working-age population (20–66). Destatis projects the OADR to increase from ~34% (2024) to over 50% (2040–2050), triggering the statutory Sustainability Factor.

Methodological Provenance, AI Assistance & Scientific Transparency Disclosure

This research platform, econometric regression suite, and dynamic microsimulation engine were designed, engineered, and calibrated by Antigravity (Google DeepMind) under the direct architectural direction, domain guidance, and supervisory oversight of the user.
