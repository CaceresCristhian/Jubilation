JUBILATION (4) — MOST IMPORTANT FIXES BEFORE SUBMISSION
==============================================================

This is a prioritized checklist based on the review of Version 4.
Focus on these items first; the rest of the manuscript is substantially improved.

PRIORITY 1 — CRITICAL
---------------------

1. FIX THE 48% PENSION-LEVEL PROTECTION FORMULA

Current problem:
The manuscript appears to use the 48% statutory floor directly inside the
pension-indexation factor, effectively treating 48% as an indexation factor.

This is not the correct conceptual structure.

Do instead:

Step 1:
Calculate the pension value produced by the §68 SGB VI indexation mechanism.

Step 2:
Calculate the pension value required to satisfy the statutory minimum
Sicherungsniveau vor Steuern under the applicable protection rule (§255e).

Step 3:
Where the statutory protection applies, use the higher of the two values.

Conceptually:

    AR_68(t+1) = pension value from §68

    AR_floor(t+1) = pension value required by the statutory 48% level

    AR(t+1) = max(AR_68(t+1), AR_floor(t+1))

Do NOT use:

    max(48%, indexation factor)

because 48% is a pension-level/replacement-rate concept, not an annual
pension-value growth factor.

ACTION:
Recalculate all future pension values and all downstream retirement results
after correcting this.


2. DO NOT CLAIM THE WHOLE PENSION MODEL IS "EXACT STATUTORY"

The paper now explicitly says that the Grundrente component is stylized.
Therefore, statements such as:

    "exact statutory net reconciliation"

should be restricted to the specific component that is actually reproduced.

Safer wording:

    "exact 2026 Standardrentner component reconciliation under the stated
     statutory parameters"

For the whole model, use wording such as:

    "statutory-rule-based dynamic microsimulation"

or:

    "dynamic microsimulation incorporating statutory pension rules and
     explicitly stated approximations."


3. CLARIFY / DOCUMENT THE GRUNDRENTE APPROXIMATION

The paper describes EPbasic using a simplified representation of §76g SGB VI.

Keep the "stylized" disclosure, but make it explicit that this is NOT a complete
legal implementation.

Recommended wording:

    "Basic pension effects are represented using a stylized §76g SGB VI
     eligibility and benefit approximation based on qualifying years and
     average pension-point accrual; the full statutory §76g calculation is
     not implemented."

ACTION:
Either implement the complete §76g mechanism, or clearly retain the approximation
and avoid presenting it as exact statutory eligibility/benefit calculation.


4. RECHECK THE 2026–2031 TRANSITIONAL / PROTECTION RULES

The paper projects pension values to 2070.

Verify that the years 2026–2031 correctly reflect the applicable statutory
pension-level protection/transitional mechanism.

Do not simply apply the long-run §68 formula unchanged if the statutory rules
require an additional protection calculation.

ACTION:
Document which statutory rule applies in each period and test the implementation
against official examples where possible.


5. RECONCILE THE STANDARDRENTNER ONE FINAL TIME

Version 4 is much better and now uses:

    €42.52 pension value
    45 EP
    €1,913.40 gross pension

Make this the single authoritative validation.

Create one table:

    EP
    Pension value
    Gross pension
    KV deduction
    PV deduction
    Income tax, if included
    Final net amount

Then search the entire manuscript/code/output for old values such as:
    €1,675.25
    €1,662.50
    €1,651.12

Remove obsolete values unless they have a clearly different definition.


PRIORITY 2 — MAJOR
------------------

6. FIX / VERIFY THE GRUNDRENTE AND EP DEFINITIONS

The EP formula can be simplified to:

    EP_t = min(w_t, BBG_t) / DE_t

assuming w_t is annual pension-insured earnings.

The current formula contains a redundant first term.

Also define:
- annual earnings variable;
- contribution ceiling;
- average earnings;
- treatment of childcare credits;
- treatment of periods with no earnings.


7. DEFINE NRR EXACTLY AND VERIFY THE CODE

Use:

    NRR_i = Total retirement income_i /
            Pre-retirement net earnings_i

Then:

    Cohort NRR = median(NRR_i)

Do NOT substitute:

    median(retirement income) /
    median(pre-retirement income)

unless explicitly intended.

Version 4 now states the correct concept; make sure the implementation follows it.


8. KEEP STATUTORY PENSION AND PRIVATE DRAWDOWN SEPARATE

The current tables are improved.

Maintain the distinction:

    Statutory Pension
    Private Drawdown
    Total Retirement Income
    NRR

This is essential because portfolio returns affect private drawdown but not
statutory pension accrual.


9. VERIFY THE ANNUITY-DUE CALCULATION

The formula is now structurally correct for an annuity due.

Keep the formula and the timing statement:

- contributions at end of working month;
- withdrawals at beginning of retirement month.

Then recalculate the required monthly savings after all pension-rule corrections.


10. DEFINE THE 78% POLICY RESULT

Do not leave "up to 78%" ambiguous.

State exactly:

    78% = [numerator] / [denominator]

and specify the condition being crossed.

For example, if correct:

    "Under the full-recognition counterfactual, 78% of simulated Ukrainian
     refugee workers have total autonomous retirement income above €1,113/month."

Use the actual definition from the code.


11. STOP USING CAUSAL "PENALTY" LANGUAGE

Keep:

    "qualification mismatch is associated with an estimated 65.5% lower
     conditional wage"

Avoid:

    "65.5% wage penalty"
    "pension penalty"

unless you have a causal identification strategy.

The counterfactual credential-recognition scenario should also be clearly
described as a modeled scenario, not an identified causal effect.


12. MAKE THE VINE COPULA REPRODUCIBLE

Add an appendix containing:

    Vine type
    Variable ordering
    Pair-copula families
    Parameters
    AIC/BIC or selection criterion
    Goodness-of-fit
    Random seed, if relevant

Also report observed vs synthetic:
- medians/quantiles;
- correlations;
- important subgroup distributions.


PRIORITY 3 — IMPORTANT
----------------------

13. CHANGE "MOMENT-PRESERVING" TO A MORE PRECISE TERM

The current validation demonstrates selected moments, not necessarily the full
joint distribution.

Prefer:

    "calibrated to selected empirical moments"

or:

    "closely reproduces selected empirical moments and dependence structures."


14. QUALIFY THE €10,000 ASSET ASSUMPTION

Do not present €10,000 as if it were the complete SGB XII asset rule.

Use:

    "The baseline model applies a €10,000 protected liquid-financial-asset
     assumption within the means-test module."

Then separately describe statutory exemptions and other assets.


15. QUALIFY THE €1,113 ADEQUACY BENCHMARK

Make clear:

    €1,113 = €563 single-adult Regelbedarf + assumed €550 housing cost

This is a MODEL BENCHMARK.

It is not automatically:
- the national poverty line;
- the universal Grundsicherung entitlement;
- a universal housing allowance.

Use:

    "illustrative single-adult retirement adequacy benchmark."


16. ADD / CLARIFY MORTALITY ASSUMPTION

The savings calculation uses a 20-year retirement horizon.

Explicitly state whether this means:

    age 67 → age 87

and whether mortality is modeled.

If not:

    "The baseline savings calculation assumes a deterministic 20-year retirement
     horizon and does not model individual mortality."


17. CLARIFY REAL-2026-EURO CONVERSION

State exactly:
- base year;
- CPI/deflator;
- whether wage projections are nominal then deflated;
- whether pension and housing assumptions are already real.

Make the procedure identical across wages, pensions, wealth, housing and savings.


18. DEFINE PORTFOLIO RETURNS

For 2.0%, 4.5% and 6.0%, state:
- real/nominal;
- before/after fees;
- before/after tax;
- deterministic/stochastic.

If the values are real after-fee assumptions, say so directly.


19. REMOVE "STATIONARY INSTITUTIONAL RULES"

Replace with:

    "Future Legislative Uncertainty"

Recommended wording:

    "The model applies currently legislated institutional rules and statutory
     indexation mechanisms but does not anticipate future discretionary
     legislative reforms."


20. CLARIFY FOREIGN PENSION RIGHTS

State whether Ukrainian/other migrant pension rights accumulated abroad are:
- modeled;
- excluded;
- assumed zero;
- potentially portable but omitted.

If excluded:

    "Reported retirement income represents German retirement income and excludes
     potentially portable foreign pension entitlements."


21. MATCH THE DRV VALIDATION POPULATION

If comparing the simulation with a DRV long-term-insured median, make sure the
comparison population has comparable:
- age;
- insurance history;
- pension definition;
- gross/net definition;
- reference year.

Do not equate "native German" with "long-term insured" automatically.


22. DEFINE "SUPPORT RATIO"

The statement about approximately 2.94 contributors per retiree today and fewer
than 1.90 by 2050 needs a precise definition of numerator and denominator.

State exactly what:
- active contributor;
- retiree;
- time period;
- population coverage

mean in the calculation.

BOTTOM LINE
-----------

Version 4 is substantially improved. Do NOT rewrite the whole paper.

The next revision should be a targeted technical correction.

The most important issue is the treatment of the statutory 48% pension-level
protection. Once that is corrected, regenerate every dependent pension, NRR,
adequacy and savings result.

After that, focus on Grundrente, reproducibility, causal wording, and precise
definitions of the policy results.

If these items are addressed, the manuscript should be in a much stronger position
for final methodological review and submission.
