What Is Explicitly Modeled & Captured
  • Wage & Part-Time Disparities: Gender wage penalty (–11.4% adjusted) and higher female part-time shares.
  • Childcare Credits (§ 56 SGB VI): Up to 3.0 Entgeltpunkte (~€126/mo pension) per child born 1992+.
  • Longevity Differential (Destatis 16. BVB): Life expectancy at 67 is ~21.5 years for females vs ~18.5 years for males.
  • Basic Pension (Grundrente § 76g): Minimum pension supplement predominantly elevating female low-earners.
Crucial Boundaries & Unmodeled Variables
  • Survivor Pensions (Witwenrente § 46 SGB VI): Up to 55–60% spousal derived pension rights are not modeled at single level.
  • Divorce Rights Splitting (Versorgungsausgleich): Equalization of pension rights between spouses upon divorce.
  • Intra-Household Wealth Pooling: Married couples often accumulate and consume wealth jointly.
  • Informal Caregiving Credits (§ 3 SGB VI): Pension points accrued while providing home care for family members.

Key Parameters & Institutional Glossary

Detailed explanation of German statutory pension formulas, adequacy thresholds, and economic definitions.

Legal Basis: SGB VI & SGB XII
GRV

Statutory Pension (GRV - SGB VI)

Primary pillar of the German pension system. Workers earn Earnings Points (EP) based on wages relative to the national average (€49,200 in 2026). At age 67, EP is multiplied by the Current Pension Value (AR = €42.15/EP), minus ~11.75% for health (KVdR) and care (PV) insurance.

NRR

Net Replacement Rate (NRR)

Measures total net retirement income as a percentage of pre-retirement net labor earnings. OECD guidelines establish 60% as the threshold for basic living standard maintenance, and 75%+ for comfortable living.

SGB

Grundsicherung im Alter (SGB XII)

Means-tested socio-cultural minimum subsistence guarantee (Chapter 4, SGB XII). Guarantees €1,113/month (€578 standard rate + €535 housing/heating costs) for retirees whose income falls below this floor and whose liquid savings do not exceed €10,000 (Schonvermögen).

S*

Required Add. Savings (S*)

The extra monthly amount a person must invest from current age until retirement age 67 to close any gap to standard adequacy (€1,450–€1,800/mo). Higher earnings and years of residence compound wealth and eliminate this required savings to €0 (Fully Funded).

4%

Private Wealth Drawdown (4% Rule)

Safe monthly withdrawal rate generated from private liquid savings and ETF portfolios. Allows retirees to generate stable passive cashflow throughout a 20–25 year retirement horizon without exhausting their underlying principal capital.

OADR

Old-Age Dependency Ratio (OADR)

Ratio of retirement-age population (67+) to working-age population (20–66). Destatis projects the OADR to increase from ~34% (2024) to over 50% (2040–2050), triggering the statutory Sustainability Factor.

Methodological Provenance, AI Assistance & Scientific Transparency Disclosure

This research platform, econometric regression suite, and dynamic microsimulation engine were designed, engineered, and calibrated by Antigravity (Google DeepMind) under the direct architectural direction, domain guidance, and supervisory oversight of the user. All demographic projections, statutory formulas (SGB VI, SGB XII, EStG), and empirical asset distributions are strictly rooted in official German institutional datasets. To ensure complete academic reproducibility and privacy compliance (SUF regulations), the synthetic microdata (N = 50,000) was generated using parametric Copula methods with exact marginal and covariance preservation.
